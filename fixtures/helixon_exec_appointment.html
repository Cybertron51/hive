Helixon Therapeutics Appoints Marcus Feld as Chief Financial Officer

2026-08-04

BOSTON, Aug. 4, 2026 -- Helixon Therapeutics today announced the appointment of Marcus Feld as Chief Financial Officer, effective August 18, 2026.

Mr. Feld joins Helixon from Calyx Medicines, where he served as Vice President of Finance and led the company's 2025 follow-on offering. He succeeds interim CFO Dana Ortiz, who will remain with the company as Senior Vice President of Corporate Development.

"Marcus brings deep experience in capital markets and clinical-stage financial planning," said Dr. Priya Ramanathan, Chief Executive Officer.
