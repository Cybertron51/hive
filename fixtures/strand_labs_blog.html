Engineering Notes: How We Cut Our Protein Design Loop from Weeks to Days

2026-08-27

By the Strand Labs platform team

Strand Labs builds de novo protein binders using a closed loop of generative design, cell-free expression, and high-throughput binding assays. This post describes how we reduced a full design-build-test cycle from about three weeks to four days.

The biggest gain came from moving expression from E. coli to a cell-free system, which removed cloning and transformation from the critical path. We now screen about 9,000 designs per cycle.

We are hiring computational biologists and automation engineers in Seattle.
