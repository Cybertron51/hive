Vantra Bio Raises $45 Million as MASH Investors Return

2026-09-23

By Sam Whitaker, Biotech Ledger staff reporter

Vantra Bio said on Monday that it had raised $45 million in a Series D round led by Halberd Growth Equity, adding to a wave of financings for companies working on metabolic liver disease.

The San Diego company plans to use the money to start a Phase 3 trial of VB-201, its oral MASH drug, after a Phase 2 study that met its primary endpoint earlier this summer.

Investors have poured money into MASH developers in 2026. Calyx Medicines and two other companies in the space have also announced financings this quarter.
