Lattice Defense to Acquire Quillfeather Labs to Add AI Code Scanning to CloudGuard

2026-09-09

SEATTLE, Sept. 9, 2026 -- Lattice Defense today announced a definitive agreement to acquire Quillfeather Labs, a startup that scans infrastructure-as-code and application code for cloud misconfigurations, for approximately $120 million in cash and stock.

Quillfeather's scanner will be integrated into Lattice CloudGuard, the company's cloud-native application protection platform, in the first half of 2027. The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.
