Helixon Therapeutics Announces $120 Million Series C Financing

2026-09-15

BOSTON, Sept. 15, 2026 -- Helixon Therapeutics, a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing gene-editing therapies for inherited cardiomyopathies, today announced the closing of a $120 million Series C financing.

The round was led by Northgate Life Sciences Partners with participation from existing investors Aldren Capital and Mosswood Ventures. Proceeds will fund the Phase 1/2 study of HLX-101, the company's lead in vivo base-editing candidate for MYBPC3-associated hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.

"This financing allows us to take HLX-101 through a first clinical readout," said Dr. Priya Ramanathan, Chief Executive Officer of Helixon Therapeutics. "We expect initial safety data in the second half of 2027."

Helixon has raised $210 million in total since its founding in 2022. The company employs approximately 140 people at its Boston headquarters.
