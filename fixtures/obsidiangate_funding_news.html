Obsidian Gate Raises $45 Million as SASE Spending Holds Up

2026-09-23

By Sam Whitaker, Cyber Ledger staff reporter

Obsidian Gate said on Monday that it had raised $45 million in a private placement led by Halberd Growth Equity, the latest in a run of financings for secure access vendors.

The Denver company plans to use the money to expand its GateEdge network and its federal business. Obsidian Gate received FedRAMP High authorization in August.
