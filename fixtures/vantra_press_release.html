Vantra Bio Closes $450 Million Series D Financing to Advance VB-201 into Phase 3

2026-09-22

SAN DIEGO, Sept. 22, 2026 -- Vantra Bio, a biotechnology company developing oral small-molecule therapies for metabolic liver disease, today announced the close of a $450 million Series D financing.

The financing was led by Halberd Growth Equity, with participation from Pinecrest Health Fund, Aldren Capital, and several existing investors. Vantra Bio intends to use the proceeds to initiate a global Phase 3 program for VB-201 in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH).

"Closing a $450 million round in this market reflects confidence in the VB-201 Phase 2 data," said Tomas Herrera, Chief Executive Officer of Vantra Bio.

In July 2026, Vantra reported that VB-201 met its primary endpoint in the 52-week Phase 2 VANTAGE study, achieving MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis in 38% of patients on the high dose versus 11% on placebo.
