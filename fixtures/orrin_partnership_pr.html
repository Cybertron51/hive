Orrin Genomics and Calyx Medicines Enter Strategic Collaboration in Liver-Targeted Genomic Medicines

2026-09-02

SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Sept. 2, 2026 -- Orrin Genomics today announced a strategic collaboration with Calyx Medicines to discover and develop RNA-targeted therapies for liver disease using Orrin's single-cell target discovery platform.

Under the terms of the agreement, Calyx will pay Orrin $30 million upfront, and Orrin is eligible to receive up to $410 million in milestone payments plus tiered royalties.

Orrin also announced that co-founder Dr. Karen Liu will step down as CEO on September 30, 2026, and that Chief Operating Officer Rafael Okonkwo will succeed her.
