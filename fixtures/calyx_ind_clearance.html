Calyx Medicines Receives FDA Clearance of IND for CLX-330

2026-08-19

CAMBRIDGE, Mass., Aug. 19, 2026 -- Calyx Medicines today announced that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration has cleared its Investigational New Drug application for CLX-330, an siRNA therapy targeting a hepatocyte gene implicated in fibrotic liver disease.

Calyx expects to dose the first participant in a Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers in the fourth quarter of 2026.
