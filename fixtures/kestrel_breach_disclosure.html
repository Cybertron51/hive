Update on a Security Incident Affecting Kestrel Identity's Support System

2026-09-18

SAN JOSE, Calif., Sept. 18, 2026 -- Kestrel Identity today disclosed that an unauthorized party accessed its customer support case management system between September 9 and September 12, 2026, using a compromised support engineer account.

The attacker viewed support case files for 134 customers, some of which contained HTTP archive (HAR) files with session tokens. Kestrel has revoked all affected sessions and notified impacted customers. The Kestrel Verify production service and customer identity data were not accessed.

Kestrel has engaged an outside incident response firm and is enforcing phishing-resistant MFA for all support staff.
