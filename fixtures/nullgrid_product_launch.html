Nullgrid Security Launches Sentinel XDR 4.0 with Autonomous Triage

2026-09-15

AUSTIN, Texas, Sept. 15, 2026 -- Nullgrid Security today announced the general availability of Nullgrid Sentinel XDR 4.0, which adds an autonomous triage agent that groups related endpoint, identity and email alerts into a single incident.

Sentinel XDR 4.0 is priced at $18 per endpoint per month for the Standard tier and $29 per endpoint per month for the Complete tier, which includes 24x7 managed detection and response. Existing customers on the Complete tier receive the triage agent at no additional cost.

"Analysts at our design partners closed incidents 41% faster in the beta," said Maya Lindqvist, Chief Executive Officer of Nullgrid Security.
