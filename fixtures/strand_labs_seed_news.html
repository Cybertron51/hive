Strand Labs Raises $28 Million Seed Round for AI Protein Design

2026-06-18

By Sam Whitaker, Biotech Ledger

Strand Labs, a Seattle startup designing protein binders with generative models, has raised a $28 million seed round led by Mosswood Ventures, the company said Thursday.

Strand plans to use the funding to expand its automated wet lab and to sign its first pharmaceutical partnership by mid-2027.
