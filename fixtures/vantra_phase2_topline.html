Vantra Bio Reports Positive Topline Results from Phase 2 VANTAGE Study of VB-201 in MASH

2026-07-14

SAN DIEGO, July 14, 2026 -- Vantra Bio today reported positive topline results from VANTAGE, its 52-week randomized, placebo-controlled Phase 2 study of VB-201 in 312 adults with biopsy-confirmed MASH and F2-F3 fibrosis.

VB-201 met the study's primary endpoint. MASH resolution without worsening of fibrosis was achieved in 38% of patients receiving 80 mg VB-201 and 27% receiving 40 mg, compared with 11% receiving placebo (p<0.001 for both doses).

VB-201 was generally well tolerated. The most common adverse events were mild nausea and diarrhea. Discontinuation due to adverse events was 4% on VB-201 versus 3% on placebo.

Vantra plans to discuss a Phase 3 design with regulators in the fourth quarter of 2026.
