Calyx Medicines Cuts 15% of Staff, Shelves Oncology Program

2026-09-10

By Jordan Mehta, Biotech Ledger

Calyx Medicines is laying off about 15% of its workforce, roughly 35 employees, and discontinuing its early-stage oncology program CLX-210, the company said Wednesday.

The Cambridge, Mass., company said the move would extend its cash runway into 2029 and let it focus on liver disease, including CLX-330 and its new collaboration with Orrin Genomics.
