# Rafael Okonkwo

**Question:** What are the latest verified developments?

Orrin Genomics has named Chief Operating Officer Rafael Okonkwo as its next CEO, succeeding co-founder Dr. Karen Liu who will step down on September 30, 2026. [1][4] The announcement came alongside a liver-disease deal with Calyx Medicines worth up to $440 million including the upfront payment. [4][5]
