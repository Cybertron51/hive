# Palo Alto Networks: competitor profile

_Every line is cited to a verified claim in Senso._

## Positioning

- Palo Alto Networks reported multiple financial figures for its fiscal year ended July 31, 2026, including $21.2 billion and $9.10 billion. [1][8]

- Palo Alto Networks released OperTraitor, an open-source LLM-powered analysis engine for Kubernetes operator risk. [6]

## Risks

- Unit 42 identified a High-severity vulnerability (CVE-2026-6389, CVSS 8.8) in IBM's Turbonomic platform. [10]
