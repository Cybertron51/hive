# Orrin Genomics

**Question:** What are the latest verified developments?

Orrin Genomics named COO Rafael Okonkwo as its next CEO. [1][4] Orrin Genomics signed a liver-disease deal with Calyx Medicines worth up to $440 million including the upfront payment. [1][4] The collaboration with Calyx Medicines focuses on discovering and developing RNA-targeted therapies for liver disease using Orrin's single-cell target discovery platform. [2][3][5]
