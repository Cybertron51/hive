# CrowdStrike: competitor profile

_Every line is cited to a verified claim in Senso._

## Positioning

- CrowdStrike was named a Leader in the 2026 IDC MarketScape for Worldwide Modern Endpoint Security for Enterprises. [10]
- IDC noted CrowdStrike's perfect score on 2025 MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise Evaluations and AAA-rated SE Labs ransomware testing. [9]
- CrowdStrike reported earnings of $5.84 billion and $1.47 billion with 25% year-over-year growth for Q2 FY2027. [3][4][5]

## Recent moves

- CrowdStrike launched Falcon Guardian, which adds agent-specific context and controls to secure AI agent activity. [2]
