# Calyx Medicines

**Question:** What are the latest verified developments?

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has cleared Calyx Medicines' Investigational New Drug application for CLX-330, a siRNA therapy. [1][2] Orrin Genomics announced a strategic collaboration with Calyx Medicines to discover and develop RNA-targeted therapies for liver disease using Orrin's single-cell target discovery platform.
