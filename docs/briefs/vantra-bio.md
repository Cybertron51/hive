# Vantra Bio

**Question:** What are the latest verified developments?

Vantra Bio reported positive topline results from VANTAGE, its 52-week randomized, placebo-controlled Phase 2 study of VB-201 in 312 adults with biopsy-confirmed MASH and F2-F3 fibrosis. [4] The Phase 2 study met its primary endpoint earlier in the summer. [5] Vantra Bio closed a Series D round to fund a Phase 3 trial of VB-201 in MASH. [1][2][3]
